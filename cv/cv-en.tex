\section*{Research Statement}
\noindent
My research develops \textbf{measurement, resource-scheduling, intelligent-operations
and security} technologies for \textbf{AI and network infrastructure}: the Internet (BGP,
RPKI, DNS, Web PKI), carrier backbones and metropolitan networks, the
\textbf{FITI / CERNET / CERNET2} national testbeds, and AI data centers. The work is
driven by national needs, built with industry, and organised along three directions that
serve one goal --- \emph{AI and network infrastructure that is observable, efficiently
scheduled, well operated and secure}:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]
  \item \textbf{AI \& Network Infrastructure Measurement.} Making production
  infrastructure observable at line rate and with zero application changes: zero-code
  distributed tracing with eBPF (SIGCOMM'23, SIGCOMM'25, ICSE'26); SRv6 probing and
  latency-bottleneck diagnosis (ICNP'25, ToN'26); flow measurement on programmable
  switches (TPDS'22); and Internet-scale measurement of Looking-Glass services, AS
  relationships and interdomain routing (WWW'26, ToN'25, ICNP'26).
  \item \textbf{AI \& Network Infrastructure Resource Scheduling.} Turning measurements
  into efficient, failure-resilient use of bandwidth and compute: reliability-aware
  routing with hybrid link protection (IPCCC'14, ICC'15, JCN'20), deployed in China
  Telecom's metropolitan networks; inter-DC WAN traffic engineering with availability
  guarantees (CoNEXT'21, ToN'22); resource scheduling and experiment services for the
  FITI testbed; centralised network
  utility maximisation (ICNP'22, ToN'25); routing algebra (INFOCOM'25); in-network
  acceleration on programmable switches (INFOCOM'24, TPDS'22); FaaS platforms for
  network functions (TPDS'23); service-function-chain embedding and scheduling
  (TPDS'26); and plug-and-play network management for enterprise clouds (NSDI'27).
  \item \textbf{AI \& Network Infrastructure Intelligent Operations \& Security.}
  Verifying configurations, diagnosing failures and detecting attacks before they
  spread: configuration verification at carrier scale (TIFS'24, CoNEXT'25, INFOCOM'26);
  inline intrusion detection on programmable switches, in production at ISPs
  (SIGCOMM'25, SIGCOMM'26); interpretable and shift-aware anomaly detection (CCS'21,
  NDSS'23); provenance-based APT and serverless Denial-of-Wallet detection (TIFS'22,
  TIFS'25, CCS'22, ToN'26); and situational awareness of Internet trust infrastructure
  --- certificates, Web PKI and routing (WWW'25, CCS'26).
\end{enumerate}

        rate. \note{SIGCOMM'25, SIGCOMM'26}
